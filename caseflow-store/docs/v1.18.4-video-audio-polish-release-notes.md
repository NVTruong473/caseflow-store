# CaseFlow Books v1.18.4

## Summary

This patch replaces the homepage introduction media with a tighter 3:34
Production walkthrough. It removes long end-of-scene narration gaps and adds a
reproducible background score that supports the Vietnamese narration without
changing commerce behavior.

## Changes

- recaptured the current v1.18.3 storefront, Buy Now, QR experience, official
  order, customer history, admin operations, and architecture story;
- reduced the walkthrough from 4:16 to 3:34 while keeping spoken phrases and
  all five chapters;
- bounded every scene tail to 0.7 seconds and retimed the long customer screen
  recording to 1.305x without accelerating the narration;
- generated an original 96 BPM G-major pentatonic score without external
  samples or third-party music rights;
- sidechain-ducked the score beneath speech and exported AAC stereo at 48 kHz;
- fixed overlapping subtitle boundaries and published synchronized WebVTT;
- added reusable background-score generation and portfolio-media publishing
  scripts;
- updated the homepage dialog, poster, portfolio package, chapters, and media
  QA report.

## Verification

- capture checks: customer order, QR experience, admin status update, cleanup,
  and zero collected console errors passed;
- media verifier: H.264 1280x720, AAC stereo 48 kHz, 214.017 seconds, 37
  monotonic subtitle cues, 14 screenshots, no sensitive artifact text;
- audio analysis: `-19.2 LUFS` integrated, `2.8 LU` range, `-4.6 dBFS` true
  peak, and no mid-video silence of 1.2 seconds or longer;
- six representative frames were reviewed without black frames, clipping, or

- runtime lint, TypeScript, build, focused browser tests, deployment, and

## Boundaries

- the narration remains synthetic and is disclosed in the portfolio package;
- the score is generated by repository code and does not use external samples;
- QR and payment boundaries remain unchanged and no real settlement is added;
- a project-owner listening pass remains useful for subjective voice and music
  preference even though objective media gates pass.
