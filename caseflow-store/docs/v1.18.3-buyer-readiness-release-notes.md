# CaseFlow Books v1.18.3

## Buyer-readiness patch

This patch hardens the final customer interactions and makes the public source
handoff reproducible before independent UAT.

## Customer fixes

- Keeps the bookstore assistant from covering or intercepting cart controls.
- Holds add-to-cart and buy-now actions until persisted cart state has hydrated,
  preventing fast clicks from being silently lost.
- Holds order cancellation until customer order state has hydrated, preventing
  a fast cancellation click from being discarded.
- Preserves the established separation between QR practice sessions and
  official orders/payments.
